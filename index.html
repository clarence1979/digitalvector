The Innovative Teacher Hub

More than 560 classroom resources for Kindergarten to Grade 12. Each one is built to work with no preparation, no student logins and, wherever possible, no internet.

What is in the Hub

  • Emergency sub plans

    Full lessons a substitute can run cold, with answer keys and every table or formula printed on the page.

  • 3D labs and simulations

    Virtual science labs and coding, finance and city simulations that open in a browser and run offline.

  • Assessment and teacher apps

    Gradebooks, progress monitoring and data collection that keep student data on the teacher's own computer.

  • Coding and computer science

    Courses from first blocks to Python, web design and cybersecurity, for teachers who are new to the subject too.

  • Digital citizenship and AI literacy

    Online safety, media literacy and how AI works, taught on paper so no tool or account is needed.

  • Professional learning for teachers

    Classroom management for new teachers, written from the experience of losing a room and getting it back.

Custom software for schools and educators

We design and build education software from first sketch to release. The same engineering runs the Hub's own apps and Teaching Tools, our subscription platform for teachers.

  • Classroom apps that run offline

    Single-file web apps that open from a shared drive or a USB stick. No install, no accounts, and student data never leaves the device.

  • 3D simulations and virtual labs

    Interactive labs for science, finance and coding, built to run in the browser on ordinary school laptops.

  • AI teaching tools

    Assistants and marking aids where the school holds its own keys, so it controls its own data and its own spend.

  • Platforms and subscriptions

    Products with sign-in, payments and administration, built and hosted for you.

Who is behind it

Clarence founded Digital Vector. He has taught for 23 years, from university lecture halls to school computer labs, and he still teaches Digital Technologies in Melbourne.

He trained as an engineer before he trained as a teacher: a Master of Engineering in telecommunications, a Bachelor of Science with Honours in electrical and electronic engineering, and a Master of Education in technical and vocational education. He is a Google Certified Innovator, Trainer and Educator, and in 2025 he was a finalist for innovation in teaching in a national teacher awards program.

  • More than 3,000 people taught

    School and university students, and engineers, managers and technicians at companies including Intel and Motorola.

  • Thirteen university textbooks

    In electronics, communications, programming and multimedia.

  • Twelve classroom tools

    From an AI marking assistant to a drone flight simulator, running on Teaching Tools.

I believe that to learn well, one has to first know the purpose of learning and be able to see the end result.
